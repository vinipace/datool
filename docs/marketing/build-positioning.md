# Datool Build positioning and campaign brief

Start with ecommerce teams answering product and order questions on their website and WhatsApp. Give them a concrete reason to try Datool: bring a question customers really ask, see an answer using their business content, and inspect what happens when the agent needs a tool or a person. This is the audience to test first; customer interviews and campaign results should decide whether to expand it.

## The offer

**Request a demo with your content and a real customer question.**

Ask for a website or help center and one recurring question. Demonstrate the answer, show its source, explain any connected action it needs, and show the handoff. A demo using published content can start before a store integration is connected. Demonstrating live stock, order data or cart changes requires that integration.

Use the same offer in the ad, landing page and follow-up. Sales ads lead to the matching sales page; support ads lead to the matching support page. Keep the existing WhatsApp demo destination.

## How to compete with Chatbase

[Chatbase's website](https://www.chatbase.co/) already promotes support, sales, product guidance, actions, procedures, testing, widgets and a helpdesk. Its [pricing page](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing) lists Shopify and WhatsApp integrations. Those features are competitive requirements, not defensible claims of exclusivity. Reviewed October 29, 2026.

Datool's angle to test is the connection between a customer conversation and the evidence behind it. Show a product question, the source or store lookup used to answer it, the human handoff, and a saved case used to check the next change. Explain that process in customer language. Keep SDKs, scorers and observability terminology in the technical follow-up unless the buyer asks for them.

Compete on a demonstration the buyer can judge. A generic feature comparison cannot establish that Datool is more accurate, easier or cheaper. Measure those claims before using them.

## The six decisions behind the copy

| Decision | Application to Datool Build |
| --- | --- |
| People | Store owners and ecommerce support leads whose teams repeatedly answer fit, stock, delivery and return questions. |
| Positioning | Help a customer take the next step, while the team can inspect the answer and take over. |
| Promise | Answer from connected business content, use configured actions and pass the conversation to a person when needed. |
| Proof | A demo using the buyer's content; visible sources and action results; a handoff; a saved test case. Fictional stories illustrate the workflow and are labeled as examples. |
| Priority | A shopper's question can arrive while the team is offline. Use that recognizable problem without inventing lost-revenue totals or deadlines. |
| Process | Connect business content and actions, try difficult questions, set handoff rules, then launch and review conversations. |

## Website application

The five existing agent pages retain their design and use more specific opening problems:

| Page | Opening message | Visitor's next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp sales | The question comes at midnight. Help them choose anyway. | Try a product question using their catalog. |
| Website sales | One question from checkout. Be there to answer it. | Try a question about fit, stock or returns. |
| WhatsApp support | “Where's my order?” Check it in the chat. | Walk through an order question and the lookup it requires. |
| Website support | Help them fix it. Right where they're stuck. | Walk through a recurring support problem. |
| Product guidance | Get users past “How do I…?” And into your product. | Try a task new users struggle to finish. |

The home page's agent section, navigation and public Build page describe these same jobs. Remove guaranteed sales, perfect-answer claims and unavailable campaigns. Explain checkout links as links to the store's checkout. Keep the requirement for connected data near stock and order claims.

## Proof to collect

Record a complete demonstration using an authorized store: the customer's question, connected source, tool result where relevant, final answer, handoff and follow-up test. Label synthetic data in screenshots. Never present an example purchase or resolution as a customer's measured result.

For a customer story, agree on the definition of resolution and the measurement window. Record conversation volume, human involvement, unresolved cases, answer quality checks and order attribution where it is actually available. Include the baseline and denominator with any percentage. Get permission before identifying the customer publicly.

## First campaign test

Test three problems separately: a product question after hours, a shopper comparing products, and a repeated order-status question. Keep each ad's offer consistent with its destination. Compare the English and Brazilian Portuguese versions within their own audiences.

Count qualified demo requests that include a business website and a specific use case. Follow them through completed demos, connected channels and active customer conversations. Use clicks to diagnose ad interest; use completed demos and active use to judge whether the promise attracts the right buyer. Do not select a winner from a handful of clicks or invent a spending budget.

After each demo, ask what the buyer currently does, what made them seek help, what they distrust about AI replies and what would justify switching. Use their words in the next revision, with permission for any attributed quote.
