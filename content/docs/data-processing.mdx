**Proposed agreement, version 25 October 2026.** This document is available for
review; publishing or reading it does not execute a contract. Contact
**vini@vinipace.com** to complete the merchant schedule and accept the agreement
in writing. Do not rely on this proposal as an already executed DPA.

## Parties and processing schedule

The service provider is **Vinicius Pacheco Furtado, operating Datool in Rio de
Janeiro, Brazil**. The merchant is the legal person identified in the written
acceptance. The merchant is the controller, or an authorized processor acting for
its controller; Datool processes merchant personal data on its behalf.

The schedule agreed with each merchant must identify the service term, enabled
features, data subjects, data categories, retention/deletion instructions,
subprocessors, locations and any required international-transfer mechanism.
Processing ordinarily includes receiving, storing, retrieving, transmitting and
deleting support conversations, contact and order information, uploaded knowledge
and associated agent execution records to provide the merchant's configured
customer service. Subjects may include the merchant's customers, prospective
customers and authorized personnel. The merchant must not instruct processing
outside the agreed scope.

Datool's independent account administration, payment, legal and security purposes
are described in the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy).

## Instructions and confidentiality

Datool will process merchant personal data only under the merchant's documented
instructions, including this agreement and authorized feature configuration,
unless applicable law requires otherwise. Where permitted, Datool will inform
the merchant of a conflicting legal requirement before processing. Datool will
inform the merchant if an instruction appears to violate applicable data-protection
law. Datool will not sell the data, use it for advertising, or train its own AI
models with it.

Access will be limited to authorized people who need it for the agreed service
and are subject to confidentiality obligations. The merchant is responsible for
lawful collection, notices, permissions, its users' access and instructions, and
its selection of suitable features and providers.

## Security and incidents

Datool will implement measures appropriate to the agreed processing and risk and
will document the applicable safeguards in the merchant schedule. That schedule
must distinguish verified deployed controls from planned work. HTTPS, permission
checks and credential encryption do not establish encryption of all stored data
or backups. This agreement does not certify Datool under SOC 2 or promise a
recovery time that has not been agreed and verified.

Datool will notify the merchant without undue delay after becoming aware of a
personal-data breach affecting merchant data, provide available information about
the incident and mitigation, and supplement it as facts become available. Datool
will assist the merchant with applicable notification, assessment and consultation
obligations, taking account of the processing and information available to Datool.

## Subprocessors and transfers

The merchant must authorize the applicable provider schedule before processing
under this agreement. Datool will impose appropriate contractual data-protection
obligations on subprocessors and remain responsible for their performance of
those obligations. The [provider register](/docs/subprocessors) identifies
infrastructure and optional connections; it does not show that every listed
provider is used by every merchant.

The advance-notice period and objection procedure for changes to subprocessors
must be agreed in the merchant schedule before this proposal is executed. Datool
will not treat a merchant's silence about this unfinished schedule as consent.
If a justified objection cannot be resolved, the parties must agree an alternative
or stop the affected processing and address return or deletion.

The parties must identify and implement any required lawful transfer mechanism
before a restricted international transfer. A link to a provider's terms is not a
substitute for completing applicable transfer clauses, annexes or assessments.
No EU-only, Brazil-only or other exclusive residency commitment is implied.

## Individual requests, return and deletion

Datool will assist with requests to exercise data-protection rights and forward
requests concerning merchant-controlled data to the merchant, except where law
requires a direct response. Assistance and identity verification will be
proportionate to the request and the information available.

At the merchant's choice when processing ends, Datool will return or delete
merchant personal data and remove remaining copies, except where law requires
retention. The merchant schedule must set the supported completion period,
export format, backup handling and verification process. Retained exceptions
remain protected and restricted to their lawful purpose. Current
[retention behavior](/docs/retention) does not supply a universal automatic
deletion deadline.

## Evidence and acceptance

Datool will make information necessary to demonstrate the agreed obligations
available and allow reasonable audits or inspections, with safeguards for other
customers' information and service security. An audit report from a hosting
provider is not a Datool audit report.

For execution, both parties must record their legal identities, authorized
contacts, date and acceptance of this version and the completed merchant
schedule. This proposal does not replace mandatory law or establish that all
requirements for a particular jurisdiction or Shopify review have been met.
