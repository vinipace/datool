This policy covers Datool's hosted service, website, and our account and support

and the instructions given to its agents. We process that workspace information
to provide the business's service. We are responsible for our own account,

A self-hosted installation is operated by its administrator. Installing Datool
does not make us the operator of its database. Ask that administrator about its

send to Datool support is covered by this policy.

## What we collect and why

| Information | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Account name, email, organization, membership, authentication and billing references | Sign-in, account administration, subscriptions and support |
| Prompts, model responses, traces, evaluations, datasets, uploaded knowledge and attachments submitted to a workspace | Run, observe, evaluate and improve that workspace's agents |
| Conversations, contact details, channel identifiers and support requests | Maintain conversation history, identify an authorized customer, route support and perform configured actions |
| Store configuration, products, prices and policies; customer and order information permitted by Shopify and the merchant | Answer product questions, verify order ownership and perform the merchant's enabled customer-service actions |
| Connection credentials and permission grants | Authenticate to services the administrator connects |
| Technical and security records, such as request information, errors and account/activity identifiers | Operate the service, investigate failures and prevent abuse |

Information comes from users, workspace administrators, connected stores and
channels, and use of the service. The enabled feature and its permissions determine
what is processed. Do not submit payment-card credentials, passwords, or sensitive
personal information unnecessary for the task. Store checkout and payment
providers handle payment details; a chat is not a payment-card collection form.

We use information to provide requested services, satisfy applicable legal
obligations, and pursue legitimate interests in running and securing Datool,
subject to applicable law and individual rights. Where consent is required, it
must be obtained for the relevant feature. The merchant remains responsible for
its customer notices, legal basis, consent choices and agent instructions.

## AI and connected services

A configured model provider receives the context needed for a request, which may
include conversation history, retrieved knowledge and tool results. Connected
channels and action providers receive the information needed for their enabled
functions. See [Service providers and subprocessors](/docs/subprocessors).

**Datool does not sell customer personal data, use it for advertising, or use it
to train Datool's own AI models.** A provider's own handling depends on the
applicable account, product and terms; this statement is not a promise about every
third-party model or service. Administrators should choose suitable providers
and minimize the personal information sent to them.

AI responses can be incorrect. Merchants should offer human support and must not
use Datool as the sole basis for decisions with legal or similarly significant
effects on individuals.

## Website analytics

Datool Cloud uses Google Analytics only after you choose **Allow analytics**.
It measures public page visits, completed account signups, checkout starts and
paid checkouts. Analytics identifiers are pseudonymous; we do not send account
emails, names, workspace content, conversations, authentication tokens or internal
workspace URLs. Public page URLs exclude query strings and fragments. Paid
checkout events include the selected plan, currency, amount and a hashed
transaction identifier. A trial or canceled payment does not count as a purchase.

Use **Analytics preferences** on any public page to change your choice. Declining
does not affect sign-in, billing or product features. Blocking analytics or
declining permission means those visits and conversions are not measured.
Google processes analytics data under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Browser storage

Authentication cookies keep account sessions signed in. The web chat uses browser
storage and visitor credentials to associate a visitor with their conversations
and preserve chat state, including an unfinished message. Shared devices can retain
that state. Clearing browser storage may remove local access or drafts; it does
not delete server-side conversation records. Merchant websites and connected
services have their own cookie and storage practices.

## Sharing, location and safeguards

Access is limited by account roles and project permissions. Authorized operators
may access information when needed for support, security, or service operation.
We use hosting, logging, email, authentication and payment services as described
in the [provider register](/docs/subprocessors). We may disclose information when
required by law or necessary to address fraud or threats to the service.

Datool is operated from Brazil, and providers may process information in other
countries. We do not offer a blanket single-country residency guarantee. A
provider's public contract alone does not establish that a particular transfer
mechanism or account setting applies to your deployment.

The hosted service uses HTTPS, access controls and application encryption for
stored connection credentials. We do not represent that every stored record and
backup is encrypted, or that Datool has a SOC 2 report. Contact us for the current
scope and evidence of safeguards needed for your use case.

## Retention and your rights

See [Retention and deletion](/docs/retention) for the current rules and their
limits. Disconnecting an integration, uninstalling a Shopify app, or canceling a
subscription is not the same as deleting stored workspace information.

Depending on applicable law, you may request information, access, correction,
export, restriction, objection or deletion, and withdraw consent where processing
relies on it. Contact **vini@vinipace.com**. We may need to verify your identity and
authority without collecting unnecessary information. If you are a merchant's
customer, contact the merchant first; we assist it with information it controls.
You may also contact the competent data-protection authority, including Brazil's
ANPD where applicable. These rights and any lawful exceptions remain governed by
applicable law.
