Datool Observe, Test, and the TypeScript and Python SDKs are open source under
Apache 2.0. The CLI and shared infrastructure also remain Apache 2.0.

Datool Build is source-available under Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2). You may use it
for your own business under that license, including a support bot whose visitors
only chat with the bot. Offering a substantial set of Build's functionality to
third parties as a hosted or managed service requires a separate commercial
agreement. This applies to free as well as paid offerings.

For the exact scope, usage examples, commercial inquiries, and existing-release
rights, read the repository's [licensing guide](https://github.com/vinipace/datool/blob/main/LICENSING.md)
and [license boundary](https://github.com/vinipace/datool/blob/main/LICENSE).
The full texts are [Apache 2.0](https://github.com/vinipace/datool/blob/main/LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt)
and [ELv2](https://github.com/vinipace/datool/blob/main/LICENSES/Elastic-2.0.txt).

The license documents accompanying your version govern that version. The Build
restriction does not change the Apache license of the SDKs or revoke rights
previously granted for Apache-licensed releases.
