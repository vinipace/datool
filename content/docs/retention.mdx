## Current retention rules

| Record category | Current handling |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary Cloud traces and associated spans/scores | Core has a 90-day trace window; Pro has 365 days. Eligible traces are removed by bounded background sweeps, so expiry is not an exact-to-the-minute deletion guarantee. This applies to eligible direct-billing and Shopify-billing organizations. |
| Traces referenced by evaluations, reviews or dataset items | Preserved as selected evidence; ordinary trace expiry excludes them. Removing the evidence references makes an otherwise eligible trace available to the expiry worker. |
| Conversations, contacts, channel messages, knowledge, datasets and evaluation/review records | The trace window does not automatically expire these records. They remain until removed through available controls or an authorized deletion request. Administrators must choose what remains necessary. |
| Ingestion queue copies | The Cloud worker sweeps completed jobs older than 24 hours and failed jobs older than 32 days, in bounded batches. Pending jobs and other application queues are separate. |
| Account, billing and security records | Kept as needed to administer the account, meet applicable recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes and investigate abuse. A removal request is reviewed for applicable obligations and exceptions. |
| Operational logs and backups | Retention depends on the deployed logging and backup configuration. There is no published universal expiry or backup-deletion guarantee. Ask for the verified schedule before relying on one. |

These are the current product rules, not a promise that every copy of personal
data disappears after the trace window. A stopped worker or cleanup backlog can
delay automatic expiry. Customer-selected services have their own retention
settings and may retain independent copies.

Self-hosted operators manage their own retention, workers, databases, logs and
backups. Installing the software alone does not enable Cloud retention or establish
a deletion schedule for that installation.

## Requesting access or deletion

Workspace administrators can use available record-management controls or contact
**vini@vinipace.com** for an authorized export or deletion request. Identify the
workspace and the scope of the request, without sending passwords, keys or an
unnecessary copy of the customer's data. We verify authority and coordinate any
necessary merchant instructions, legal exceptions and applicable deadlines.

Customer requests received from Shopify are recorded for handling. Receipt or
acknowledgement of a webhook is not proof of a completed export or erasure. A
completed request must account for the relevant conversations, traces, stored
files, queues, exports and other retained copies. Records held under a lawful
exception must be limited to that purpose. Where backup copies cannot be removed
individually, their isolation, expiry and treatment after restoration must be
addressed in the applicable deletion plan.

## Leaving the service

Disconnecting Shopify stops use of that connection. Uninstalling the app or
canceling a subscription does not automatically erase historical records. Request
return or deletion of the relevant workspace data when ending service. Clearing
browser storage removes local state, not server records.

A merchant that needs a fixed maximum retention period for all customer data must
agree a supported schedule before using the service for that requirement. Do not
treat a future retention feature or an uninstalled backup plan as an active control.
See the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy) and
[merchant data-processing agreement](/docs/data-processing).
