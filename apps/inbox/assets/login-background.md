# Login background

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the Datool Inbox welcome screen. Bundled locally as `login-background.jpg` so startup does not depend on an image host. Converted to JPEG for app size; no remote image requests.

## Generation prompt

Create one photorealistic editorial background image for Datool Inbox, a premium mobile customer-support app. Portrait 9:16 composition, full bleed, no typography, no logos, no UI, no frames. A candid warm late-afternoon scene in a contemporary outdoor cafe: two colleagues sitting across a small travertine table, natural hands reaching toward a coffee cup and a phone; a closed silver laptop and subtle olive-green upholstery, linen clothing, human warmth and quiet focus. Crop people at the shoulders, no prominent faces. Main visual action and objects in the upper half and upper right; leave the lower 45 percent visually calm with dark forest-green shadow and soft texture for white headline and login controls to be overlaid in code. Natural golden sunlight, tactile film grain, rich green shadows, terracotta highlights, crisp yet relaxed, premium independent magazine photography. Realistic anatomy and objects. No call-center headset, no neon, no illustrations, no fake screen text. Produce the photo alone.

## Google sign-in artwork

`google-signin.png` is the unmodified 4x iOS light icon button from [Google’s official branding assets](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip). The UI shows only its center G inside a matching white button, preserving the original logo colors and aspect ratio. [Branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
